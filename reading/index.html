🦉 Why do we learn reading?

Reading is the key that opens every other subject. A child who reads well can teach herself almost anything. Writing lets us share what we think, clearly, with anyone.

  • 🚏Reading signs and maps
  • 🍳Following a recipe
  • 💌Writing a card to family
  • 📖Getting lost in a good story
