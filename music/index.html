🎵 Music

Play, listen, read notes, keep the beat and meet the orchestra.

🦉 Why do we learn music?

Music is sound put together in patterns. Learning it trains careful listening, memory and counting, and it is a way to share feelings without words.

  • 🎂Singing Happy Birthday
  • 💃Dancing to a beat
  • 🎬Film music that feels scary or happy
  • 🚑Sirens and doorbells
