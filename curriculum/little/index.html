🐣 Foundations curriculum

Year 1 · Little learner, about ages 3–5 (pre-K to kindergarten skills).

🌱 How we teach the youngest

  • Play first. Short lessons of 5–15 minutes, lots of talk, songs and real objects. Most of the day is off-screen.
  • Screens with a grown-up. About 20 minutes a day on OakCrest apps (Little Letters, Zoom Town, Little Musicians), always together.
  • A letter sound a week in a proven synthetic-phonics order, taught through words; numbers through counting real things.
  • Read aloud every day: picture books and a nursery rhyme to learn each week.
  • Move and play outside every day, with small jobs at home and a virtue word each week.
  • Check gently. One weekly check (Got it, Almost, Not yet) and a few skill checks; "not yet" means repeat, never hurry.
