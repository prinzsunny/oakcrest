📘 Curriculum

Year 1 · Big learner · October 2026 – October 2027. The Year 1 courses lesson by lesson, the advanced apps by subject, the machine of each week, and every module: what it uses, how often, how it is checked and the year's goals. Print this page as the school's course descriptions.

🗓️ Open the week-by-week plan
Reading first

The year's top priority is fluent, joyful reading. The read-aloud happens every day and is never skipped.

Depth before speed

Hard problems are worth sitting with. Fast, easy work means the level is too low; tears after five minutes mean too high.

Mastery before moving on

Advance a book when practice is 85% or better without help. 'Not yet' means slow down.

Math first, screens later

The first block of the day is math on paper. Website practice is short (about 10 minutes) and comes later.

Praise effort, never 'smart'

Name the effort and the strategy. Mistakes are information, not failure.

Character before intelligence

Five virtues this year, a weekly big question, and one act of service a week.

Mathematics

Course area: Mathematics
Resources
  • Beast Academy 2A → 2B, then 2C (Guide + Practice)
  • Primary Grade Challenge Math (Zaccaro): challenge problems
  • Daily oral mental math (no calculator)
  • Soroban class practice
  • Life of Fred: Apples (a funny math story, read together twice a week)
  • Math games: Tangrams, Set, KenKen, Pentominoes, Blokus
How often
45 minutes every school day, math first; 10 minutes of mental math; 2–3 challenge problems a week; a Solution Notebook for hard problems.
How we teach it
Read the Beast Academy guide together, then work the practice book. Try for 10 minutes before asking for help. Write the week's hardest problem in the Solution Notebook: what you tried, what worked and what it taught you.
How we check
Weekly check, a monthly timed challenge set (practice, not a test), and the 85% rule before moving on. No competitions are required this year; Math Kangaroo Level 1 (March) is optional, for the experience.
Year goals
  • Beast Academy 2B complete and 2C under way
  • Addition and subtraction facts fluent to 20
  • Adds and subtracts within 100 mentally
  • Explains place value to 1,000 with real understanding
  • Solves 2-step word problems by drawing them
Sequence
Term 1 (Oct–Dec)Place value: ones, tens, hundreds; comparing and ordering; addition with regrouping and strategies.
Term 2 (Jan–Mar)Subtraction with regrouping; expressions and parentheses; balance equations; problem-solving strategies (draw it, guess and check, work backward, tables). Finish 2B.
Term 3 (Apr–Jun)Measurement and units; time and money; flexible strategies and estimation; odds and evens.
SummerMath games and puzzles a few times a week.

Learn that words have roots: Latin and Greek pieces carry meaning, so unfamiliar words can be decoded. Latin words are learned with pictures and connected to the English words that come from them. This builds a strong vocabulary for later reading and tests.
How we check
Unit reviews at 90% before moving on; derivative detective games.

Sequence

Term 2Family, nature, animal and action words; derivative detective.

Character, ethics and philosophy

Course area: Health / Life Skills
Resources
  • The year's five virtues: honesty, kindness, courage, self-control, gratitude
  • The Book of Virtues (Bennett); Aesop's fables; a biography each month
  • A weekly big question (philosophy for children)
  • Feelings words
How often
A virtue moment daily; a big question once a week (15 minutes); one act of service a week.
How we teach it
Name virtues when they happen. Mistakes follow name, understand, repair, forgive. Big questions are answered with reasons ('I think… because…'), by listening to another view, and by changing your mind only when evidence and sound reasoning show the other view is true.
How we check
She names the 5 virtues with examples; gives reasons in discussion; uses feelings words.
Year goals
  • Names 5 virtues and gives an example of each
  • Tells the truth when it is uncomfortable
  • Gives a reason for her view and listens to another
  • Names 15–20 feelings
Sequence
Term 1Honesty, kindness, self-control; gratitude at Thanksgiving.
Term 2Courage; owning mistakes; fairness.
Term 3All five virtues together; service; a reflection on the year.
