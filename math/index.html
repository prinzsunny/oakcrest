🦉 Why do we learn math?

Math is how we count, share, measure and solve puzzles. It helps us think step by step, spot patterns and check that things are fair. We use it every day, often without noticing.

  • 🍪Sharing snacks fairly
  • 🛒Paying at the shop
  • ⏰Knowing when it's bedtime
  • 🧁Measuring for baking
