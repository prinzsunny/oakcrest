How to use it
  1. Choose a browning setting with − and +. Each step adds 30 seconds.
  2. Press Press the lever, or drag down on the picture. At the bottom the switch closes and the wires begin to heat.
  3. Watch the power, the time and the energy, and watch the bread brown. When the time is up, the spring pops the toast.
  4. Tap any part, or use ◀ ▶, to read what it does. Look inside lifts the case away.
