How to use this model
  1. Watch the dots: they are the refrigerant. Hollow dots are gas, filled dots are liquid, and the colour shows the temperature, red for hot and blue for cold.
  2. Press Open the door. Watch the thermometer climb, and see when the thermostat starts the compressor.
  3. Read the table: where is the pressure high, and where is it low?
  4. Tap any part, or use ◀ ▶, to zoom in and read what it does. Take it apart lifts the lid, pulls the coil off and opens the compressor.
