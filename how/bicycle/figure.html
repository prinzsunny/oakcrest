How to use this model
  1. Press Pedal (hold it to keep going), or drag the crank round with your finger.
  2. Choose a chainring (34 or 50 teeth) and a rear sprocket. Watch the derailleur move the chain.
  3. Read the numbers: teeth ÷ teeth gives the gear ratio, the number of wheel turns for one pedal turn.
  4. Press Coast and stop pedaling: the wheel keeps turning and the freehub clicks.
  5. Tap any part, or use ◀ ▶, to zoom in and read about it. Look inside slides the cassette off to show the pawls.
