How to use this model
  1. Press Lower (or drag the pole's long end down). Watch the bucket sink into the river and fill.
  2. Press Raise. The counterweight lifts the full bucket, and it is tipped into the basin.
  3. Read the panel: the torque (weight × distance) on each side, and the force you must supply.
  4. Slide the counterweight along the pole and observe how the work moves between lowering and lifting.
  5. Tap any part, or use ◀ ▶, to examine it. Take it apart separates the pieces.
