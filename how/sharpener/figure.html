How to play
  1. Press Turn the pencil (or hold it down, or drag across the picture).
  2. Watch the blade shave a curl of wood, and the pencil's end become a cone.
  3. Count how many turns until it is sharp. Then press New pencil.
  4. Tap any part, or use ◀ ▶, to zoom in and read what it does. Take it apart slides the pieces off along their axes.
